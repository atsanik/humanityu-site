Delete Your HumanityU Account

You can request deletion of your HumanityU account in the app or by email. This page explains the active-account deletion flow and where copies may remain.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open HumanityU and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile. If you want a copy first, use Download My Data.
  3. Tap Delete My Account, read the confirmation, and confirm when ready.

Wait for the deletion result. If the app reports that deletion or device cleanup could not finish, retry or contact support. Sign Out does not delete your account.

Option 2 — By email

Email support@humanityu.app with the subject Delete My Account from the email address on your account. We will verify your request, process the account deletion, and report the outcome. Response times may vary with available support coverage.

What is deleted

The in-app flow deletes your authentication account and linked database records through the current account-deletion workflow. It also attempts to clear account-owned data from the device where you perform deletion. This does not certify erasure of media or copies held outside those records.

What may be retained, and for how long

Database backups, copies saved on other devices, and records held by service providers may persist after deletion from the active database. Some records may be retained where required by law. We are verifying backup and provider retention periods and have not completed an isolated restore-and-deletion reconciliation. Contact support if you need help with a specific erasure request.
