Delete Your HumanityU Account

Unpublished draft for review. The retention controls described below require backend verification. This draft is not an effective policy. An effective date and any required advance notice must be confirmed before publication.

You can permanently delete your HumanityU account and the personal data associated with it at any time. This page explains how, and what is removed.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open HumanityU and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile. If you want a copy first, use Download My Data.
  3. Tap Delete My Account, complete the recent sign-in check, read the confirmation, and confirm when ready.

Wait for the deletion result. If the app reports that deletion or device cleanup could not finish, retry or contact support. Sign Out does not delete your account.

Option 2 — By email

Email support@humanityu.app with the subject Delete My Account from the email address on your account. We verify account ownership without asking for your password or verification codes, and complete deletion of active account data within 30 days of a verified request. The limited retention exceptions below apply separately.

What is deleted

Your account record, email, username/display name, profile and conservation region, your environmental measurements and journals, your action reports (including any notes and places you typed), and your community posts and comments.

What may be retained, and for how long

Reported-content evidence: deleting your account removes ordinary reported-content copies associated with it. Deleting an individual post or comment may leave a necessary private moderation copy while its case is open. We review open cases at least every 30 days, remove unnecessary evidence earlier, and remove resolved-case evidence within 30 days after resolution and any applicable, documented appeal period ends. Repeated reports do not automatically extend this period.

Specific legal requirements: a documented legal requirement can justify a minimal private excerpt or record. It is reviewed at least every 30 days and removed when that requirement ends.

Recovery backups and deletion records: residual backups are restricted to disaster recovery. Minimal private deletion identifiers and times help us reapply deletions before a restored service returns to use. They contain no deleted content and are retained only as necessary to cover recoverable copies. We do not promise a numerical backup-erasure or deletion-record cleanup deadline until provider recovery limits are confirmed.

Copies you export or save outside HumanityU remain under your control. The Privacy Policy retention section explains these exceptions.

Provider service logs: Supabase separately processes service/security logs that may include email addresses, IP addresses or request metadata. Their retention follows the provider’s applicable lifecycle and is separate from the deadlines for active account data and reported-content evidence.
