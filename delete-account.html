Delete Your HumanityU Account

You can request deletion of your HumanityU account and its linked active records. This page explains how to make the request, what the deletion service removes, and the limits that apply to other copies.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open HumanityU and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile. If you want a copy first, use Download My Data.
  3. Tap Delete My Account, read the confirmation, and confirm when ready.

Wait for the deletion result. If the app reports that deletion or device cleanup could not finish, retry or contact support. Sign Out does not delete your account.

Option 2 — By email

Email support@humanityu.app with the subject Delete My Account from the email address on your account. We verify requests and handle them within applicable legal deadlines. Never send your password or a sign-in verification code to support.

What is deleted

The deletion service removes your authentication account and linked database records through the database's deletion rules. These include your profile, environmental records and community content where linked to the account. Wait for the app's result; deletion and device cleanup can fail and may need a retry. The result does not certify erasure from backups, other devices or external providers.

What may be retained, and for how long

Database backups, service-provider records, copies saved on other devices and records required by law may remain after active account deletion. Their retention and deletion rules are separate from the in-app deletion process. This page does not promise that every copy or log is immediately removed or anonymous. Contact support@humanityu.app about a particular record or deletion request.
