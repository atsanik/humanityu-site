# Website changes, November 24, 2026 (read before publishing)

This branch (`claude/true-website-nov24`) changes the website so every page says what HumanityU does as it will launch: United States only, English only, the whole app 18+, a $2.99 download plus a subscription for the community and the Crisis Map, and no promise the app does not keep. Nothing is published until this branch is merged into `main`. Merging publishes at once.

**This file is not linked from any page, but everything on `main` is public.** Delete this file in the same merge (or right after), or accept that it can be read at humanityu.app.

Pages that carry a date now say "Last updated: November 24, 2026" (decision 53: publish the new terms right away, with the date they changed). Numbers like "decision 21" refer to your decision list in the session record.

No script, font or tracker was added. Page structure, style and links are kept; one link was added (Terms of Service in the home page footer) and a few outside links were added (listed under "Before you publish").

---

## Before you publish: check these (from your phone)

1. Open each new outside link once: report.cybertip.org; the phone number 1-800-843-5678 (NCMEC's public line); Apple's standard licence page (terms section 19); the privacy pages of Resend, Cloudflare, Apple, Google and GitHub (privacy section 4). If one does not open the right page, tell the Code session.
2. The child-safety page says that apparent child sexual exploitation is reported to NCMEC's CyberTipline, as United States law requires. If your tipline registration is not done yet when something must be reported, use the public form, as your email steps say.
3. The automatic hold (3 separate reporters), Cloudflare's sign-up check and the store age signal are written with "can" or "may", so they stay true whether each is switched on or off. If any of them is NOT in the build you submit, tell the Code session and that line comes out.
4. The pages state $5 a month, $60 a year and a $2.99 download. If a store cannot offer exactly these prices, the pages change with them.
5. If the build you submit still checks the internet connection through Google's address on iPhone (the connectivity change not merged), add one sentence to privacy section 4: "On iPhone, the app checks for an internet connection by contacting a Google address, which sees your network address and nothing else."
6. Terms section 22 and privacy section 12 promise 14 days' notice of a material change. These new terms take effect today (decision 53), which is fine only because no public member has signed up yet.

## Your choices kept as they were (change them if you want)

- Terms section 2, the parent sentence ("If you are under the age of majority where you live ... a parent or legal guardian ..."): kept unchanged. The age of majority is 19 in Alabama and Nebraska and 21 in Mississippi, so it still means something for some 18 to 20 year olds. Keep or drop: your call.
- "We will remove the account and its data" for someone under 18 (terms section 2, privacy section 8, child-safety page) and "within 30 days" for deletion by email: both are done by you, by hand. The app itself only signs a refused account out and deletes nothing.
- The EU, EEA and UK consumer paragraphs in the terms and the GDPR line in the privacy policy are kept. They are not untrue, and they do no harm for a US-only launch.
- Not checked and left as they were: the home page crisis cards and the numbers bar, the calculator feature texts, the support page's notification times and export answers, and the sources page's list of additional sources.

## What the pages do NOT say (on purpose)

- No response time anywhere (decision 34): "One person reads reports by hand."
- No promise to preserve evidence, no "child-safety reports are handled first", no urgent alert, no "under 18" report choice, no 24-hour pause, no "I agree to the community rules" step. None of these is built yet.
- The creator offer is described only as a term (on the terms page), never advertised. Google does not allow promoting it outside the app, so the home page does not mention it.
- No grace-period number (Apple 3 days, Google its shortest; decision 47): the terms say only "a short grace period".
- Retention after an account is deleted is written as "may be kept for up to 30 days" and "for up to one year" (decisions 54, 32 and 26). That is true today and stays true once the locked copy is built.
