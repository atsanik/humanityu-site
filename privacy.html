Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

1Introduction

HumanityU ("we," "us," or "our") is an environmental impact tracking platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use the HumanityU mobile application (iOS and Android) and related services (the "Service").

By using HumanityU, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Our commitment: HumanityU does not sell your personal data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with data brokers. Your environmental measurements belong to you.

2Data We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we store your email address for authentication and account recovery and your username as a display name. You may also choose a region for your profile.

2.2 Environmental Measurements

When you use calculators, we store measurements you enter (gallons saved, kg CO₂ reduced, trees planted) to build your own impact record. These are figures you report yourself, and we describe them that way wherever they appear.

2.3 User-Generated Content

When you post or comment in the Community feed, we store the text you submit and the associated display name. A post may also contain the region or location text you choose to enter. Posts and comments are screened before publication. Photo and video uploads are disabled in this version.

2.4 Usage Data

HumanityU does not use an advertising or analytics SDK. Our backend stores limited operational data, including per-user request counts, the endpoint used, and the time window, to enforce rate limits and protect the Service. Service providers may also process standard request metadata. We do not use advertising IDs or cross-app tracking.

2.5 Location

HumanityU does not request your device's location permission. You may select a region in your profile or enter location text in a community post; we store those values when you submit them. Map tile requests also reveal the area of the map you view to the tile provider. These are different from collecting your device's GPS location.

3How We Use Your Data

  • App functionality — authenticating your account, displaying your impact record, and showing the community feed.
  • Content moderation — automated screening of community posts and comments. The screening request can include your display name, content, region, and any location text you submitted. AI gateway retention and training terms are still being verified.
  • Push notifications — streak reminders, milestone alerts, and weekly digests. Disable in Profile → Notification Settings at any time.
  • Offline sync — temporarily caching failed submissions in device local storage for automatic retry.

We do not sell your data to data brokers or use it for advertising. We also process limited operational records to secure and support the Service.

4Third-Party Services

Supabase
Database · Authentication

Powers our backend database (PostgreSQL) and user authentication. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest. It is not end-to-end encrypted — your community content is processed by our systems for safety moderation and app functionality, and is readable by authorized infrastructure. Supabase is SOC 2 Type II compliant.

Supabase Privacy Policy →
AI Moderation Gateway
AI Content Moderation · Conservation Advisor · Crisis News Curation

The configured gateway handles community moderation, advisor questions, and crisis news requests. For community moderation, it can receive your display name, submitted content, region, and optional location text. The company operating the configured gateway, model route, retention period, and training terms have not yet been verified. Avoid including sensitive personal information in posts or advisor questions.

OpenTopoMap / OpenStreetMap
Map Tile Rendering

The Crisis Map tab loads topographic tiles from OpenTopoMap servers. Tile requests include viewport coordinates (the map area you view) and standard network information such as your IP address. Viewport coordinates are not your device's GPS location. Data is available under CC-BY-SA 3.0.

Apple APNs / Google FCM
Push Notifications

Push notifications are delivered via Apple APNs (iOS) and Google FCM (Android). Your push token is stored solely for delivering notifications you have opted into. Revoke at any time via device settings or Profile → Notification Settings.

5Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data with service providers described in Section 4, as required by law, or as part of a business transfer subject to applicable notice requirements.

What other people can see: Your username and the content, region, or location text you choose to post in the community are visible to other signed-in users. The app does not display your account email as a separate public field, but other people can see any personal information you put in your username or posts. Your account-owned environmental measurements are not automatically shared to the community.

6.2 Account Deletion

You can delete your account from inside the app, in Profile. The in-app flow deletes your authentication account and linked database records. It does not certify immediate removal from backups, other devices, or external providers. If you would rather not use the in-app route, email support@humanityu.app with the subject "Delete My Account"; we will verify the request and report the outcome. You can also delete individual posts and comments within the app at any time.

You can request an export of your data before deleting — see Section 9.

7Security

  • All data in transit encrypted with TLS 1.2+
  • Data at rest encrypted by Supabase managed infrastructure
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) policies restrict access to account-owned records and permit community records according to feature visibility
  • Sign-in uses an email address and password; email verification codes are used for registration and account recovery

8Children's Privacy

HumanityU is intended for users aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

HumanityU is rated for a teen audience because it includes user-generated content (community posts and comments). Direct messaging is disabled in this version. We collect account email and username, optional region or location text, content you submit, and limited operational rate-limit records. We do not use advertising tracking. HumanityU is a paid app: a single purchase, with no in-app purchases and nothing further to buy. Text posts are automatically screened before publication; photo and video uploads are disabled until moderated media review ships. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has provided us personal information, contact support@humanityu.app and we will delete it promptly.

9Your Privacy Rights

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction — update inaccurate data via Profile or by contacting us
  • Deletion — delete your account in the app, or request it (Section 6.2)
  • Portability — request export of your environmental measurement data
  • Withdraw consent — revoke notification permissions via device settings

California (CCPA): You have rights to know, delete, and opt out of sale. We do not sell personal information.

EU/EEA (GDPR): Legal basis is performance of contract. You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10Cookies & Tracking Technologies

HumanityU is a mobile application and does not use browser cookies. The app uses AsyncStorage (device-local only) for onboarding state, offline submission queue, and notification preferences. Some locally queued submissions are transmitted to our backend when you choose to sync or the app retries them. We do not use advertising identifiers, analytics SDKs, or any third-party tracking.

11International Data Transfers

Data may be processed in the United States and European Union where our service providers maintain infrastructure. We are verifying the applicable transfer mechanisms and provider terms before wider release; contact us if you need details about a particular provider.
