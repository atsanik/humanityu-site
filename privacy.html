Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1Introduction

HumanityU ("we," "us," or "our") is an environmental impact tracking platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use the HumanityU mobile application (iOS and Android) and related services (the "Service").

Our commitment: HumanityU does not sell your personal data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with data brokers. Your environmental measurements belong to you.

2Data We Collect

2.1 Account Information

We use your email address for authentication, account recovery and account communications, and your username as your public display name. You may also choose regional information for your profile.

2.2 Environmental Measurements

When you use calculators, we store measurements you enter (gallons saved, kg CO₂ reduced, trees planted) to build your own impact record. These are figures you report yourself, and we describe them that way wherever they appear.

2.3 User-Generated Content

We process the text you submit in posts, comments and action reports. This can include report notes, a public display name, and location or regional information you enter. Text is screened before publication. Photo and video uploads are disabled in this version.

2.4 Usage Data

The app does not use an advertising analytics SDK or cross-app advertising tracking. Our backend processes account-linked request counts, endpoint names and time windows to enforce rate limits. Service providers may also process network addresses, request metadata and security logs when handling network requests. These operational records are different from advertising tracking.

2.5 Location

HumanityU does not request your device's location permission. You may select regional information in Profile or type a location into a report or community post. We process those values when you submit them. Map tile requests identify the area of the map you view. These values are different from reading your device's GPS location.

3How We Use Your Data

  • App functionality — authenticating your account, displaying your impact record, and showing the community feed.
  • Content moderation — automated screening of submitted text. Screening requests may include report notes, typed location, and the public name, content or region associated with a post, comment or action summary.
  • Notifications — streak reminders, milestone alerts, and weekly digests. Change app preferences in Profile → Notification Settings, or disable notifications in your device settings.
  • Offline sync — temporarily caching failed submissions in device local storage for automatic retry.

We do not sell your data to data brokers or use it for advertising. We also process operational records to secure and support the Service.

4Third-Party Services

Supabase
Database · Authentication

Powers our backend database (PostgreSQL) and user authentication. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest. It is not end-to-end encrypted — your community content is processed by our systems for safety moderation and app functionality, and is readable by authorized infrastructure. Supabase is SOC 2 Type II compliant.

Supabase Privacy Policy →
AI screening services
Automated text processing

The screening service requests a Google Gemini model through an external AI gateway. It receives the submitted text described in Section 3; that text can include personal information. Advisor requests, where available, can also include your question and earlier conversation text. Provider retention, security logging and training practices depend on the applicable provider arrangement and settings; this policy does not promise immediate deletion or absence of provider training. Keep medical information, financial account details, government identifiers and other sensitive personal information out of text you submit.

OpenTopoMap / OpenStreetMap
Map Tile Rendering

The Crisis Map tab loads topographic tiles from OpenTopoMap servers. Tile requests identify the map area you view and include standard network information such as your IP address. The map area is not your device's GPS location. Data is available under CC-BY-SA 3.0.

Device notification services
Reminders and alerts

The app uses your device's notification facilities to schedule reminders and alerts. Change app preferences in Profile → Notification Settings and system permissions in your device settings.

5Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data with the services described in Section 4, as required by law, or as part of a business transfer subject to applicable notice requirements.

What other people can see: Your public name and the text, region or location you share in community content can be visible to other users. Account-owned environmental records are not automatically published; an action summary you choose to post can disclose information shown in its sharing preview. The app does not display your sign-in email as a separate community field, but personal information that you put in a name or post can be visible to others.

6.2 Account Deletion

You can request account deletion in Profile. The deletion service removes the authentication account and linked database records through the database's deletion rules. It does not certify immediate erasure from backups, other devices or external providers. Wait for the result; if deletion or device cleanup cannot finish, retry or contact support. You can also email support@humanityu.app with the subject "Delete My Account" from your account email. We verify requests and handle them within applicable legal deadlines. See account deletion details.

You can request an export of your data before deleting — see Section 9.

7Security

  • All data in transit encrypted with TLS 1.2+
  • Data at rest encrypted by Supabase managed infrastructure
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) policies restrict access to account-owned records and permit community records according to feature visibility
  • Email and password sign-in; email verification is used for registration and account recovery

8Children's Privacy

HumanityU is intended for users aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

HumanityU includes user-generated text such as posts and comments. Direct messaging and photo/video uploads are disabled in this version. Data processed by the Service includes the account details, optional regional or location information, submitted content and operational records described in Section 2. We do not use advertising tracking. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has provided us personal information, contact support@humanityu.app so we can investigate and handle deletion.

9Your Privacy Rights

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction — update inaccurate data via Profile or by contacting us
  • Deletion — delete your account in the app, or request it (Section 6.2)
  • Portability — request export of your environmental measurement data
  • Withdraw consent — revoke notification permissions via device settings

California (CCPA): You have rights to know, delete, and opt out of sale. We do not sell personal information.

EU/EEA (GDPR): Legal basis is performance of contract. You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10Cookies & Tracking Technologies

The app uses device storage for onboarding state, drafts, queued submissions and notification preferences. Locally queued submissions may be transmitted to our backend when you sync or the app retries them. Keeping a private draft is different from requesting a submission. We do not use advertising identifiers or cross-app advertising tracking.

11International Data Transfers

Service providers may process data outside your country. Processing locations and transfer safeguards depend on the service and applicable agreement. Contact support@humanityu.app for information about a particular processing activity or a privacy request.

12Changes to This Policy

Material changes will be notified via in-app notification or email at least 14 days before they take effect. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
