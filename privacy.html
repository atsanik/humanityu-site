1Introduction

HumanityU is made by Atsanik LLC, a limited liability company formed in Wyoming, United States ("we," "us," or "our"). Atsanik LLC is responsible for (the "controller" of) your personal data. HumanityU is an environmental impact tracking platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use the HumanityU mobile application (iOS and Android) and related services (the "Service"). HumanityU is offered in English, to adults 18 and over, in the countries where the App Store or Google Play sells it. Availability depends on your country and your store. It is not offered in the European Union at this time.

Our commitment: HumanityU does not sell your personal data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with data brokers. Your environmental measurements belong to you.

2Data We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account: Email address (to sign you in and to send your sign-up and password-reset codes) and Username (display name on community posts).

2.2 Environmental Measurements

When you use calculators, we store measurements you enter (gallons saved, kg CO₂ reduced, trees planted) to build your own impact record. These are figures you report yourself, and we describe them that way wherever they appear.

2.3 User-Generated Content

The community (for subscribers) supports text posts, comments and likes only. Posts and comments are checked by a filter for English words and phrases before they appear; it reads English only and will miss things. There are no photo or video uploads and no direct messages. We also keep the reports and blocks you make, reports other members make about your posts, comments or account, and our records of what we decided about a report and when.

2.4 Usage Data

We collect no analytics or usage tracking. HumanityU contains no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK, and the app sends us no telemetry. On Android, Google's billing library, which handles purchases, may send Google its own usage information (Section 4). Errors are handled inside the app and are not transmitted to us or anyone else. To enforce rate limits, our backend keeps a count of your requests to some features, with the feature name and the time window. We do not collect device fingerprints, advertising IDs, browsing history, or cross-app tracking data.

2.5 Location

HumanityU does not request location permission and never accesses your device's location. If you choose a region in your Profile (from a list) or type a place into an action report, we store what you enter.

2.6 Age Check

Before the app can be used, it asks for your birth date. The date is checked on your phone and is never saved or sent to us. The phone keeps only whether the check was passed and on which day, or the time of a refusal. The app may also ask Apple or Google for your age range, only to apply our minimum age; the range is used on your phone and is never saved or sent to us.

2.7 Subscription

If you subscribe, we keep your subscription's status from your phone's app store: the plan, its status and dates, and the store's own reference for it. To match the store's records to your account, we give the store a random code instead of your account ID. We never see your card or payment details. If you use a creator code on Android, we keep a record that your account used it.

3How We Use Your Data

  • App functionality — authenticating your account, displaying your impact record, and showing the community feed.
  • Content moderation — a filter for English words and phrases checks community posts and comments before they are published, and the one person who runs HumanityU reads reports by hand. We may hide a post or comment that several members report until we look at it. Your posts are not sent to any AI service.
  • Subscriptions — checking with Apple or Google whether your subscription is active, so the app can open the community and the Crisis Map to you.
  • Sign-up protection — a security check that stops automated sign-ups (Section 4).
  • Email — sending your sign-up and password-reset codes, and answering messages you send us.
  • Notifications — reminders, milestone alerts, and weekly digests, scheduled on your device. Disable in Profile → Notification Settings at any time.
  • Offline sync — temporarily caching failed submissions in device local storage for automatic retry.

We never use your data for advertising, profiling, selling to third parties, or any purpose not listed above.

5Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data only with sub-processors (Section 4), as required by law (for example, a report of apparent child sexual exploitation to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children), or in a business transfer (with notice and policy continuity guaranteed).

What other people can see: Your username and anything you post to the community feed are visible to other members who can open the community. Your email address is never publicly visible. Your environmental measurements are private to you and are not shown to anyone else.

6.2 Account Deletion

You can delete your account from inside the app, in Profile. Deletion removes your account and the data linked to it, apart from the limited copies described in the retention table above (reports about you, our records of decisions on them, and a locked copy of what was reported; routine backups; and our sign-in provider's security logs). Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it first in the subscription settings of your App Store or Google Play account. If you would rather not use the in-app route, email support@humanityu.app with the subject "Delete My Account" and we will complete it within 30 days. You can also delete individual posts and comments within the app at any time.

If someone reports a post or comment you shared, we keep a private copy for the person who reviews reports, even if you delete it. We delete that copy once the reports on it are dismissed and no action on it stands. If you delete your account while reports about you are open, the retention table above says what may be kept and for how long.

You can request an export of your data before deleting — see Section 9.

  • You sign in with your email address and password; signing up and resetting a password need a one-time code sent to your email address

8Children's Privacy

HumanityU is intended for adults aged 18 and older. It is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

HumanityU is for adults 18 and over because it includes user-generated content (community posts and comments). Before the app can be used, it asks for a birth date and turns away anyone who gives one under 18. If that person is signed in, the app signs the account out of the phone; it deletes nothing, and the phone gives the same answer for 24 hours. The check goes by the answer given and cannot prove anyone's age. There are no direct messages and no photo or video uploads. We collect only email, username and what you choose to enter — no device location, no behavioral tracking, and no advertising. If you are a parent or guardian and believe someone under 18 has provided us personal information, contact support@humanityu.app. We review every such report and may suspend or delete the account. If the person is under 13, we delete their personal information, apart from what a report about a child's safety requires us to keep for up to one year (Section 6).

9Your Privacy Rights

  • Deletion — delete your account in the app, or request it (Section 6.2)

Wherever you live, you can make any of these requests by writing to support@humanityu.app. We answer verified data requests within 30 days.

10Cookies & Tracking Technologies

HumanityU is a mobile application and does not use browser cookies of its own. The app uses AsyncStorage (device-local only) for onboarding state, the age check result, offline submission queue, and notification preferences. This data is never transmitted to third parties. On Android, Cloudflare's sign-up security check (Section 4) may leave a small record in the app's web storage between checks. We do not use advertising identifiers, analytics SDKs, or any third-party advertising tracking. Everything the app stores on your device is needed to provide the app you asked for or to keep it secure, so we do not ask for separate consent.

11International Data Transfers

Your data is stored in the United States (Supabase, US East region). Requests may pass through, and be processed by, our providers' networks in other countries on the way, including content-delivery and edge servers near you. Because your data is processed in the United States, it may be accessed by courts, law enforcement and national security authorities there under US law. If you are in the UK or Switzerland, you can ask us at support@humanityu.app how a particular provider protects your data when it is sent to the United States.
