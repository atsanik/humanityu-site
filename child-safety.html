Child Safety Standards

HumanityU — published standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)

Our commitment

HumanityU has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation. No content or conduct that sexualizes, exploits, or endangers minors is permitted anywhere in the app — including posts, comments, usernames, and profile content.

Prohibited content and conduct

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form
  • Sexualization of minors, real or fictional
  • Grooming, sextortion, solicitation, or attempts to contact minors inappropriately
  • Sharing personal information of minors
  • Any use of HumanityU to facilitate child exploitation

How we enforce

  • Adults only (18 and over): HumanityU is for adults. Before the app can be used, it asks for a birth date and turns away anyone who gives one under 18. If that person is signed in, the app signs the account out of the phone; it deletes nothing. The phone then gives the same answer for 24 hours without asking again. The check goes by the answer given and cannot prove anyone's age. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, write to support@humanityu.app. We review every such report and may suspend or delete the account.
  • Word filter: every community post and comment is checked by a filter for English words and phrases before it appears. It reads English only and will miss things. There are no photo or video uploads.
  • Reports read by hand: HumanityU is run by one person, who reads reports by hand and can hide content or suspend an account. We do not promise a response time.
  • Automatic hold: a post or comment reported by three separate members can be hidden automatically until that person looks at it.
  • In-app reporting and blocking: every post, comment and member can be reported, and members can be blocked, from the Post options or Comment options button in the app.
  • No direct messages: there is no private messaging between members.
  • Account action: when we find content that breaks this rule, we remove it and suspend the account responsible.

Reporting to authorities

If we find apparent child sexual exploitation on HumanityU, we report it to the CyberTipline of the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), as United States law requires.

How to report a concern

In the app: tap Post options or Comment options, then Report post, Report comment or Report member.

By email: support@humanityu.app with the subject line "CHILD SAFETY". One person reads this mailbox, and we cannot promise when we will reply.

If a child is in danger now, call your local emergency number first (911 in the US and Canada, 999 or 112 in the UK).

You can also report child sexual exploitation directly to NCMEC's CyberTipline at report.cybertip.org or 1-800-843-5678.

Compliance

We publish these standards for Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy and Apple's App Review Guidelines (1.2 — User-Generated Content). They describe our policy, what the app does and how to report a concern; they are not a certification of legal compliance.
